## 💡 The Core Problem
Smallholder farmers face severe financial distress:
- **Panic Distress Sales:** Perishable crops (tomatoes, onions) are dumped locally at ₹2–₹5/kg due to lack of shelf-life assessment.
- **Logistics Blindspot:** Farmers do not know whether traveling to a higher-paying mandi 30 km away will yield net profit after freight costs.
- **Digital Divide:** Existing solutions are heavy, complicated, and lack low-end mobile accessibility.

---

## 🚀 Key Features
1. **📸 Multimodal Produce Grader (Powered by Google Gemini 1.5 Flash):**
   - Take or upload a photo of harvested crops.
   - Instantly estimates quality Grade (A/B/C) and remaining shelf-life days.
2. **🚛 Smart Mandi Arbitrage:**
   - Real-time comparison between Local, District, and Metro mandis.
   - Calculates dynamic transport costs and net-in-hand profit.
3. **🌱 Khet & Subsidy Planner:**
   - Pre-sowing multi-crop guidance and direct access to KVK seeds and government subsidy schemes.
4. **📱 Low-End Mobile Optimized:**
   - Lightweight, responsive design with clear Hindi-English dual support.
